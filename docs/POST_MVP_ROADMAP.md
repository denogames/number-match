# Number Match — Prioritized Post-MVP Roadmap

## Phase 1: LiveOps & Seasonal Events (High Priority)
- **Weekly Tournaments**: Asynchronous weekly score races where players compete on deterministic tournament seed boards for coin and badge rewards.
- **Holiday & Seasonal Themes**: Limited-time festive themes (Halloween, Winter Wonderland, Cherry Blossom) with custom tile artwork and soundscapes.
- **Push Notification Retention**: Gentle daily reminder at user-selected preferred local time to maintain daily challenge streaks.

## Phase 2: Cosmetic Tile Sets & Expanded Economy (Medium Priority)
- **Tile Customization Packs**:
  - Pixel Retro (8-bit digits and chiptune audio)
  - Neon Synthwave (glowing neon lines with synthesizer chords)
  - Handcrafted Wood (tactile wooden blocks and subtle wood clicks)
- **Coin Sinks**: Spend earned gameplay coins in the cosmetic shop to unlock tile themes and particle trails.

## Phase 3: Power-ups & Extended Game Modes (Medium Priority)
- **Bomb Booster**: Clears any selected 3x3 neighborhood of cells, removing obstacles.
- **Swap Booster**: Allows player to swap the positions of two adjacent active numbers.
- **Endless Zen Mode**: Continuous relaxed matching with automatically flowing rows and no failure conditions.
- **Bounded Master Mode**: Strict fixed-board puzzle challenge where no "Add Numbers" is permitted; each board is guaranteed to clear to exactly zero cells.

## Phase 4: Social & Cross-Platform Cloud Sync (Long-Term)
- **Cloud Save Sync**: Optional Sign in with Apple / Google Play Games to sync coins, statistics, and theme unlocks across multiple devices.
- **Friend Leaderboards**: Challenge friends to beat your daily challenge completion times.
